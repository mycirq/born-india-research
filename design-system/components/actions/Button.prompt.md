The standard action control — ink-filled by default, with a hairline secondary and a text-link "quiet" variant.

```jsx
<Button href="mailto:hello@mybornindiaresearch.com">Get in touch</Button>
<Button variant="secondary" href="#how">How it works</Button>
<Button variant="quiet">Read the methodology</Button>
```

Sizes `sm | md | lg`. Hover darkens primary to laterite; secondary darkens its border to ink. No scale or lift on press. One primary button per view — this brand does not stack CTAs.
